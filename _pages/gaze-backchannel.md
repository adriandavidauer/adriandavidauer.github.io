We could set someone behind a screen looking at the input from a camera and have multiple people speak to the robot. We would need the wizard to wear gaze detection goggles and the robot needs to be able to direct its gaze. So in this way we could steer the gaze in the way a human would do that, with our ASD solution and without gaze steering.
